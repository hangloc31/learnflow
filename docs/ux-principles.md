# UX Principles

The site has one job: turn a visit into a conversation (consultation, trial class, placement
test). Every section earns its place by answering a visitor question and offering a next step.

## First 5–10 seconds (hero contract)
The hero answers, without scrolling: **what** (modern English education center), **for whom**
(children → adults, structured paths), **why care** (outcomes and care, not just classes),
**next step** (one primary CTA: explore programs; one secondary: get guidance). Trust markers
sit in the hero, not hidden below.

## Section purposes (homepage)
| # | Section | Visitor question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Hero | Is this for us? What do I do next? |
| 02 | Audience selector | Which program fits my child/me? |
| 03 | Trust/proof | Is this center established? |
| 04 | Differentiators | Why here and not elsewhere? |
| 05 | Programs | What exactly can be learned? |
| 06 | Learning journey | Is there a system, or just classes? |
| 07 | Classroom experience | What does a day here feel like? |
| 08 | Teachers | Who will teach us? |
| 09 | Outcomes | What results do learners get? |
| 10 | Testimonials | Do other parents/students trust it? |
| 11 | Events | Would my child enjoy being here? |
| 12 | Knowledge hub | Do they teach beyond marketing? |
| 13 | FAQ | What do I still need to know? |
| 14 | Final CTA | How do I start? |

## Interaction principles
- One primary CTA per viewport. CTAs are specific ("Tìm chương trình phù hợp"), never generic ("Submit").
- The audience selector is the site's signature interaction: ≤ 2 taps from "who is it for?"
  to relevant programs. No backend required.
- Motion clarifies; it never decorates. Entrances ≤ 600ms, fire once, respect reduced motion.
- Mobile is the primary experience: sticky CTA after hero, 44px targets, no shrink-the-desktop.

## Voice
Warm, direct, credible. Vietnamese-first copy. Short sentences. No edu-jargon, no
exclamation inflation, no fear-based marketing ("thời điểm vàng" used sparingly and honestly).

## Anti-patterns (removal criteria)
Repetitive equal card grids, centered text walls, decorative gradients, autoplay media,
sections that exist "because websites have them", fabricated proof (stats/testimonials/
credentials). Anything hitting these gets cut, not restyled.
